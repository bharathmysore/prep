- The Bhava Chalit uses KP-style Placidus cusps calculated from the birth date/time, approximate Mysore city-center coordinates, and calibrated to the supplied Aquarius 17°18′ sidereal ascendant.
- The Sun is close to the estimated 3rd cusp and is the most cusp-sensitive natal planet.

- Vimshottari dates are approximate. The Moon longitude is supplied only to one arcminute; lowest-level period boundaries can shift by several days.
- Public online ephemerides checked for July 2026 agree on the slow planets’ sidereal signs but sometimes disagree on exact degrees. Transit interpretations should therefore be treated as sign-level unless recalculated with one consistent ephemeris.
